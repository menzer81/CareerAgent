About This Opportunity

The Senior Manager, Software Engineering is responsible for leading one or more engineering teams delivering complex, high-impact software systems. This role combines strong technical leadership, people management, and operational accountability to ensure reliable delivery, scalability, and alignment with business priorities.

You will drive execution across multiple initiatives while developing engineering talent, shaping architecture decisions, and ensuring operational readiness across systems.

Responsibilities

Leadership & People Management

Lead, coach, and mentor Software Engineers and Engineering Leads across one or more teams 
Drive talent strategy, including hiring, retention, succession planning, and performance management 
Set clear expectations, goals, and development plans aligned with organizational priorities 

Delivery & Execution

Own delivery of major initiatives spanning multiple systems or domains 
Ensure predictable execution through strong planning, prioritization, and resource allocation 
Drive Agile execution discipline, including sprint planning, backlog management, and delivery metrics 
Proactively identify risks, dependencies, and bottlenecks; drive resolution 

Technical Leadership

Guide architectural decisions and ensure alignment with enterprise standards and long-term strategy 
Ensure systems are scalable, resilient, and secure, with appropriate observability and operational readiness 
Review and influence design, implementation, and testing approaches across teams 
Drive adoption of modern engineering practices (CI/CD, DevOps, automation, AI-assisted development, etc.) 

Operational Excellence

Own production health, including incident response, root cause analysis, and remediation 
Ensure readiness across monitoring, alerting, dashboards, and runbooks prior to releases 
Drive continuous improvements in reliability, performance, and cost efficiency 

Stakeholder Management

Partner with Product, Architecture, and Business stakeholders to define roadmaps and priorities 
Align engineering execution with business objectives and compliance requirements 

Qualifications

Experience

5-8+ years of software engineering experience 
3+ years managing engineering teams 
Proven track record delivering complex, distributed systems in production environments 

Technical Skills

Strong understanding of software architecture and system design 
Experience with modern development practices (Agile, CI/CD, DevOps) 
Experience with performance, scalability, and reliability engineering 

Leadership & Execution

Demonstrated ability to scale teams and deliver through others 
Strong decision-making capability in ambiguous and high-pressure environments 
Ability to balance technical depth with business priorities 

Success Measures (First 6-12 Months)

Consistent on-time delivery of key initiatives with clear visibility into progress 
Improved system reliability, performance, and operational readiness 
Strong team engagement and capability growth 
Clear alignment between engineering execution and business outcomes
