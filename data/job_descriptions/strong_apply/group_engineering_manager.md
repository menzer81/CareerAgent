**Location:** Remote (US)
**Level:** Manager

## About Rivergate SaaS Holdings

Rivergate builds property management and financial systems software for mid-market real estate operators. Our engineering organization spans two products and roughly 30 engineers.

## About the Role

We're hiring a Group Engineering Manager to lead two engineering teams (accounting systems and platform enablement), reporting directly to the VP of Engineering. You will manage engineering managers as well as senior individual contributors.

## Requirements

- 8+ years software engineering experience, 5+ years engineering management
- Experience managing multiple engineering teams and other managers
- Strong background in cloud platforms (AWS) and containerization/Docker
- Experience leading large-scale training or enablement initiatives for engineering organizations (100+ engineers)
- Track record of reducing product defect backlogs while maintaining delivery commitments
- Property management, SaaS, or financial systems industry experience preferred

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience organizing AWS or cloud transformation readiness programs
- Experience creating company-wide technical learning/mentoring programs
- .NET / C# background
