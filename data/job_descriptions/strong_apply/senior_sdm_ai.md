# Senior Software Development Manager — AI-Enabled Engineering

**Company:** Northlake Digital Solutions
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Level:** Senior Manager

---

## About Northlake Digital Solutions

Northlake is a growth-stage SaaS company delivering managed services and compliance software for mid-market enterprises. Our engineering org is ~40 people across three product lines.

## About the Role

We're hiring a Senior Software Development Manager to lead a team of 8-12 engineers building our compliance automation platform. This is a **manager-of-managers** role — you will oversee a team lead in addition to individual contributors.

## Requirements

- 8+ years of software engineering experience, including 5+ years of engineering management
- Demonstrated experience as a manager of managers
- Strong experience with .NET, C#, and Azure cloud infrastructure
- Experience driving GitHub Copilot or similar AI-assisted development adoption across a team
- Track record of leading teams through organizational change or team turnaround situations
- Experience with SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance programs
- Vendor management and budget planning experience

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience training or enabling AI tooling adoption across a department
- AWS or Azure certifications
- Experience with distributed/offshore engineering teams
- Background in managed services or B2B SaaS

## Compensation

- Base: $170,000-$195,000
- Bonus: 15% annual target

## Why Northlake

- Fast-growing, private-equity backed SaaS company
- Strong AI adoption culture — engineering leadership is actively investing in Copilot and coding agents
