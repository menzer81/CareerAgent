# Director of Engineering — Platform Organization

**Company:** Solace Health Technologies
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Level:** Director

---

## About Solace Health Technologies

Solace builds compliance and workflow software for healthcare providers. Our engineering organization has grown to 120 engineers across eight teams.

## About the Role

We are hiring a Director of Engineering to own our Platform organization — five teams and roughly 45 engineers, led by five engineering managers who report to you. You will report to the VP of Engineering and sit on the engineering leadership team.

## Requirements

- 10+ years software engineering experience, including 7+ years engineering management
- Prior Director-level or above experience required
- Demonstrated manager-of-managers experience at 40+ engineer scale
- P&L responsibility for a multi-million dollar engineering budget
- Deep healthcare or highly regulated industry experience (HIPAA, SOC 2, HITRUST)
- Executive stakeholder management and board-level presentation experience
- Strong track record of scaling engineering organizations through hypergrowth

## Preferred Qualifications

- VP of Engineering experience
- Experience with AI/LLM product strategy
- Cloud architecture background (AWS or Azure)
- Experience standing up new engineering offices internationally

## Compensation

- Base: $220,000-$260,000
- Bonus: 20% annual target
- Significant equity grant
