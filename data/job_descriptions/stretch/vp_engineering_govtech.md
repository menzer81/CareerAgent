# VP of Engineering — GovTech Platform

**Company:** Civitas GovTech
**Location:** Washington, DC (Hybrid)
**Level:** VP

---

## About Civitas GovTech

Civitas builds mission-critical software for state and federal transportation agencies. Our engineering organization spans 200+ engineers across a dozen product teams.

## About the Role

We are hiring a VP of Engineering to own engineering strategy, architecture direction, and organizational leadership across the entire technology organization. You will manage a team of directors and senior managers, and report directly to the CTO.

## Requirements

- 12+ years software engineering experience, including 8+ years senior engineering leadership
- VP-level or above experience required
- Extensive manager-of-managers experience across multiple layers of engineering leadership
- P&L and multi-department budget ownership
- Government, transportation, or public sector technology experience
- Board and executive stakeholder presentation experience
- Track record of large-scale organizational transformation initiatives

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with AI/LLM adoption at an organizational level
- Cloud migration leadership (AWS, Azure)
- SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / FedRAMP compliance program experience
- Experience managing distributed teams across multiple time zones
