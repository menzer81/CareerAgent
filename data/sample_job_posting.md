## About Meridian Financial Technologies

Meridian is a Series C fintech company processing $15B+ in annual payment volume across 40 countries. We build the infrastructure layer that lets banks, neobanks, and fintechs launch financial products faster. Our 300-person engineering team operates across San Francisco, New York, and London.

We are profitable, growing 3x year over year, and preparing for an IPO within the next 18 months.

## About the Role

We're looking for a Director of Engineering to lead our Platform & Infrastructure organization — a 45-person team across four groups:

- **Developer Experience** (10 engineers): Internal tooling, CI/CD, developer portal
- **Core Infrastructure** (15 engineers): Cloud platform, networking, Kubernetes fleet
- **Data Platform** (12 engineers): Streaming ingestion, data warehouse, observability
- **Security Engineering** (8 engineers): AppSec, cloud security posture, compliance

This is a **manager-of-managers** role. You'll directly manage 4 engineering managers, who each lead their own teams. You'll report to our VP of Engineering.

## What You'll Do

- Own the platform engineering roadmap and ensure it enables 300+ product engineers to ship fast and safely
- Partner with Product, Finance, Legal, and Security on cross-functional initiatives

- Lead our platform reliability program targeting 99.99% uptime
- Own headcount planning and recruiting for the org
- Present quarterly engineering investment proposals to the executive team
- Lead our AI tooling strategy — we are actively exploring LLM-based internal tooling
